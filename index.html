Our mandate
"Therefore, leaving the discussion of the elementary principles of Christ, let us go on to perfection."
Hebrews 6:1

We help every member lay a firm biblical foundation, build a daily and weekly rhythm of prayer and practice, understand and endure seasons of spiritual dryness, and press on toward deeper, stable union with God — not as an escape from ordinary life, but as the deepening of it.

Who we are

A word from the founder

I'm Lucky Mahlasela, founder of Branch of Righteousness. For more than thirty years I've walked a contemplative Christian journey — one that grew into my own pursuit of a deeper walk with Christ, and now into this ministry.

Beyond the Veil: Entering the Mysteries of God is the written form of that pursuit, and this community exists to walk it out together, one honest week at a time.

Hebrews 6 · The starting line

Six foundations, laid in order

Everything we build rests on these. Not beginner topics to move past — the ground floor everything else stands on.

01

Repentance from dead works

Turning the soul away from what is lifeless, toward the living God.

02

Faith toward God

Teaching the heart to lean upon Him who cannot fail.

03

The doctrine of baptisms

Cleansing, identification, and sacred belonging.

04

Laying on of hands

Blessing, commissioning, and spiritual transmission.

05

Resurrection of the dead

Lifting the eyes beyond the grave.

06

Eternal judgment

All things finally weighed in the light of God's righteousness.

Foundations come before fire; obedience before rapture; surrender before union. You can't skip to the deep things — these six principles are the deep things, laid straight.

Our framework

Walking through Beyond the Veil

A journey into Christian mysticism

Beyond the Veil
Entering the Mysteries of God

LUCKY MAHLASELA

Our teaching and discussion follow the arc of this book — from the six foundations, through the witness of the Desert Fathers and medieval mystics, into daily practice, the dark night of purification, and mystical union with God.

It is not a detour from Christ, but a fuller entrance into the reality every believer has already received in Him. Ordinary believers, in ordinary places, pursuing an extraordinary, ordinary walk with Christ.
